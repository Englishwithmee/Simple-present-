Grammar Theory

When do we use the simple present?

FACTS

I'm Italian.
I live in New York.
He doesn't work at a restaurant.

REGULAR ROUTINES

I work every day.
We go to the movies on the weekend.
They get up at 10 on Sundays.

Affirmative (+) and negative (−)

FormI / you / we / theyhe / she / it
Affirmative (+)We work in a hospital.
I teach English.
They have a new car.		Laura works in an office.
He teaches Japanese.
She has an apartment.
Negative (−)We don't work in a school.
I don't teach French.
They don't have a garden.		Paul doesn't work in a store.
He doesn't teach in a school.
She doesn't have a dog.

Remember: After don't or doesn't, use the base form of the verb. ✓ She doesn't work. ✗ She doesn't works.

Spelling rules: he / she / it

Most verbs: +s

work → works
live → lives

Consonant + y: change y to -ies

study → studies
try → tries

-sh, -ch, -x, -s: +es

finish → finishes
watch → watches

Irregular verbs

go → goes
do → does
have → has
